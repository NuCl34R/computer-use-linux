At the bottom-right tiny text 'WAYLAND · X11 · MCP'. Professional product branding, unique visual signature. No third-party logos, no penguin mascot, no robot, no generic purple-blue gradients, no overloaded HUD, no stock-photo laptop, no browser chrome.
